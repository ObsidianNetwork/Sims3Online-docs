# Code of conduct

Sims3Online welcomes respectful, constructive participation in issues, reviews,
discussions, and contributions. This page is a concise guide; the
[repository Code of Conduct](https://github.com/ObsidianNetwork/Sims3Online/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
is the governing policy for its pledge, standards, scope, enforcement, and attribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep criticism specific to the work, evidence, and impact.
- Accept corrections and clarify misunderstandings in good faith.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information or private conversations.
- Respect project licensing and safety boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized language or imagery,
sexual attention or advances of any kind,
sustained disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.
Do not pressure contributors to share game files, credentials, or personal saves.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the community leaders privately at
[security@obsidiannetwork.au](mailto:security@obsidiannetwork.au), the address named in the
governing policy. Do not post conduct reports in public issues or discussions. Share only
the information needed to explain what happened and where; avoid forwarding private
details more widely than necessary.

For a suspected vulnerability, follow the private reporting instructions in the
[security policy](../security.md). For ordinary technical support, use
[support and issue reporting](support-and-reporting-issues.md).

## Enforcement

Community leaders review reports and may remove content, request behavior changes, or
restrict participation as appropriate under the governing policy. Handle reports
respectfully and privately, apply standards consistently, and avoid retaliating against
reporters.

| Situation | Constructive response |
|---|---|
| A review finds a defect | Describe the affected behavior and how to verify a fix. |
| A technical disagreement escalates | Return to constraints, evidence, and alternatives. |
| Private information is posted | Do not amplify it; notify maintainers privately. |
| Someone reports misconduct | Keep the report private and treat the reporter respectfully. |

Related: [contribution workflow](contribution-workflow.md), [support and reporting](support-and-reporting-issues.md),
[security](../security.md), and the [full Code of Conduct](https://github.com/ObsidianNetwork/Sims3Online/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
